
# Privacy Policy — PRISM BREAK

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

PRISM BREAK ("the game") is developed by QuestByte Labs. This policy explains what data is processed when you play the game.

## Summary

- We do not operate our own servers or directly collect your personal information.
- The game displays ads through **Google AdMob**. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information as described below.
- Your game progress stays on your device.

## Data stored on your device

Progress and settings (unlocked chapters, best scores, checkpoints, volume and quality settings) are saved **only on your device**, in the game's private storage.

Uninstalling the game deletes this data, and Settings → Reset Progress allows you to erase your progress at any time.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display:

- Occasional full-screen ads between runs (never during gameplay).
- An ad when you return to the game after being away.
- Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for extra marbles.

To serve, limit, measure and personalise ads, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

- Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID.
- Approximate location derived from your IP address.
- Diagnostic and performance information about the app and ads.
- Interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Your choices

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** On first launch, the game displays Google's consent form where applicable. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
- **Personalised ads:** You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, through your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through advertisements in this game, please contact us so we can help you request deletion from Google.

## Other services

No analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or other third-party data services are used. Unity Analytics and Unity Crash Reporting are disabled in this game.
